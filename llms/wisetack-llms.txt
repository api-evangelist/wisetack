# Wisetack


> Wisetack is an embedded consumer financing platform for service businesses. We let home service pros (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, fencing, garage doors, flooring, pest management, and more) offer customers a simple monthly payment option for larger purchases — delivered via text or link, with approval in minutes. Payment options through Wisetack are provided by Wisetack's lending partners; all financing is subject to credit approval.


Wisetack works with 50+ field service management and vertical software platforms (e.g. Housecall Pro, Jobber, JobNimbus, Thryv, Thumbtack) to embed financing directly into existing merchant workflows via API.
